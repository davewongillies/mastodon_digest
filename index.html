I don’t talk about it much, but I grew up evangelical Christian. But not always. For the first decade or so we were mainline Protestant.

Some time in the late 90s / early 00s, evangelism swept through parts of southeast and East Asia. The prosperity gospel has its hooks on a rapidly wealthy part of the world, as the aspiring middle class all associated becoming Christian with becoming richer.

For various reasons (Palestine, Iraq) I left church in 2004 and never returned. I disagreed with the fundamentally politically wrong and polarizing nature of the false preachers of the evangelical branches of the faith.

My last straw was when my church attempted to collect donations in the aftermath of a war.. for the perpetrators of the violence. My old pastor in Singapore has been widely decorated for his services to Zionism. My government prosecuted anti-Iraq War protestors.

At the same time, I saw a growing assault on secularism by the Singaporean Chinese Christian evangelicals who were yelling about culture wars, but they were the ones putting up anti-gay banners outside their churches, running conversion therapy camps, yelling about how it was unacceptable that queer people can be acknowledged.

I saw how their disingenuous overlap of faith and pro-family often coincided with the ‘pro-family’ ideals of the state, and evil organizations like Focus on the Family were allowed to operate and impart their foreign, homophobic, transphobic policies in the name of support and religious counselling.

I don’t believe there is anything we can learn from an organization founded by a man who wants us to beat our children.

After two decades of seeing what is often incorrectly described as a Christian nationalism wreak havoc across the world: this shit isn’t national in any way. It’s a pan-global cult that works in exactly the same undesirable ways everywhere.

Find the people with the most political power. Fuse it with money. Create an in-group, out-group dynamic. Scapegoat sexual and racial minorities. Be as cruel as possible while pretending to be the face of morality and family. And claiming to be the persecuted.

I’m so tired of it.
